ENCHANTED BOOKSHOP V4.26.4 — READING STATUS INDEPENDENCE

This fixes a deeper modeling bug discovered during real testing:

OWNING A BOOK DOES NOT MEAN YOU WANT TO READ IT.
WANTING TO OWN A BOOK DOES NOT MEAN YOU WANT TO READ IT.

NEW READING STATUS:
• No reading status

BEHAVIOR:
• New collection/manual entries default to No reading status.
• Want-to-Own-only discoveries use No reading status.
• Existing blank reading statuses display as No reading status.
• Book Detail no longer falls back to Want to Read.
• Want to Own is shown separately in Book Detail.
• A No-reading-status book can be deliberately added to TBR with “Add to TBR.”
• TBR remains only books explicitly marked Want to Read.
• Owned family/inherited books can remain owned without being placed on TBR.
• Adding an edition/copy does not invent a reading intention.
• Power Search can filter No reading status.

NO AUTOMATIC MIGRATION OF EXISTING WANT-TO-READ BOOKS.
The app cannot safely know which old Want-to-Read records were intentional, so it leaves them alone.
