ENCHANTED BOOKSHOP V4.27.3 — SERIES CANDIDATE FILTER PATCH

REAL-WORLD RESULT FROM V4.27.2
Internet retrieval finally worked, but the candidate list contained unrelated
books from the author's wider catalog.

THIS PATCH TARGETS THAT EXACT FAILURE
• Carries the matching Open Library series label forward as evidence.
• Scores candidates by actual series evidence, numbering, exact series-title
  match, or an already-known local series book.
• Rejects weak author-catalog-only candidates instead of treating every book by
  the author as part of the requested series.
• Adds stronger generic parsing for series numbers embedded in structured
  labels.
• Reclassifies sub-1 numbered volumes and explicit novella/prequel/companion
  cues as Novella / Extra.
• Diagnostics now show Accepted internet candidates.

IMPORTANT
This patch does NOT hard-code A Good Girl's Guide to Murder or Holly Jackson.
The filtering rules are generic so Series Discovery can work across the user's
library.

TRUTH RULES RETAINED
• No automatic ownership.
• No automatic Want to Read.
• No automatic Want to Own.
• Nothing saves until user confirms the lineup.

NO SQL REQUIRED.
DO NOT CLEAR SITE DATA.
