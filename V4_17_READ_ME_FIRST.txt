Enchanted Bookshop V4.17 — Reading Statistics & Insights

Built from the passed V4.16 Customizable Home Dashboard checkpoint.

NEW
- Reading Insights page on desktop navigation and mobile More menu
- Sessions logged, reading days, minutes read, pages logged, books read, average rating
- Current-year finished-book snapshot
- Most logged reading mood and longest reading session
- Genre patterns from journaled books
- Reading mood distribution
- Rating constellation
- Responsive phone + desktop layouts

DATA
No new database schema is required. Insights are calculated from existing library, reading-status, rating, genre, finish-date, and Reading Journal data.

Pages logged are calculated from page-progress entries per book; they are an estimate of pages represented by journal progress, not a claim about unlogged reading.
