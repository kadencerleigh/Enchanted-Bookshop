ENCHANTED BOOKSHOP V4.27.15 — CONFIRMED LINEUP LIBRARY MATCHING FIX

ROOT CAUSE
The saved Series page built a per-series bucket from owned catalog records whose stored
series name exactly matched the confirmed series name. A real cataloged copy could therefore
be absent from that bucket even when its title exactly matched a confirmed lineup entry.

TARGETED FIX
• Confirmed lineup catalog matching now checks explicitly owned local catalog records by
  normalized title when the per-series bucket misses them.
• A local record only counts as cataloged when owned === true.
• Existing "owned but needs cataloging" flags remain supported for lineup entries with no
  actual catalog record.
• Generic series ownership checks now reuse the same truth-aware matcher.
